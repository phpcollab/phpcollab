## Decision Matrix

### **Choose BOUTIQUE market if:**

✅ You're bootstrapping (limited funding)
✅ You want profitability ASAP (Year 1)
✅ You want to minimize risk
✅ You're comfortable with higher volume, lower ARPA
✅ You want easier customer acquisition
✅ You can build simple, focused product
✅ You want to build sustainably

### **Choose MID-MARKET if:**

⚠️ You have significant funding ($100K+)
⚠️ You're okay losing money Year 1
⚠️ You want higher ARPA (bigger customers)
⚠️ You can handle more complex sales
⚠️ You want to compete directly with Basecamp
⚠️ You have enterprise ambitions later

### **Choose ENTERPRISE if:**

❌ You have $500K+ in funding
❌ You have experienced enterprise sales team
❌ You can wait 2-3 years for profitability
❌ You want to build a unicorn
❌ You're comfortable with high CAC ($500-2,000)
