# PHPCollab SaaS Validation Checklist
## Go/No-Go Decision Framework

**Purpose:** Determine if building PHPCollab SaaS is worth your 6-12 months of runway
**Timeline:** 2-3 months of validation BEFORE building anything
**Investment:** $500 maximum + your time

---

## Phase 2: Landing Page + Pre-Sales (Weeks 4-6)

### Week 4: Create Landing Page

**Design & Build:**
```
□ Sign up for Carrd.co ($9/year) or similar
□ Write headline: "Project Management for Boutique Teams - $39/mo"
□ Write subheadline emphasizing savings vs competitors
□ Add pricing: Starter ($39) and Professional ($69)
□ Create comparison table vs Basecamp/Asana
□ Add testimonial quotes from interviews (with permission)
□ Set up email capture form
□ Add Stripe payment button for pre-sales
□ Test on mobile and desktop

Pre-sale offer:
"Founding Member - Pay $99 once, get lifetime access
(normally $69/month = $828/year)"

Time: 6-8 hours
Cost: $9-50 depending on platform
```

**Copy to Write:**
```
□ Main headline (focus on pain point from interviews)
□ 3-5 key benefits (based on what people said they need)
□ Pricing section (clear, simple)
□ FAQ section (address objections you heard)
□ "How we're different" section (vs current solutions)
□ Social proof (# of people on waitlist, interview quotes)

Use actual quotes from interviews:
"We're paying $150/month for Basecamp and only 4 of our
12 people actually use it" - Sarah, Marketing Agency Owner
```

**Technical Setup:**
```
□ Domain name (if you have one) or use subdomain
□ SSL certificate (usually included)
□ Email collection (ConvertKit free tier or Mailchimp)
□ Stripe account setup for payments
□ Google Analytics or Plausible for tracking
□ Connect email to autoresponder

Cost: $0-20
```

## DECISION POINT (End of Week 6-8)

### GO / NO-GO CRITERIA

**✅ STRONG GO - Build This (All 3 must be true):**

```
□ 10+ people pre-paid $99 (from 30 interviews + landing page)
□ 50+ email signups on landing page
□ 15+ people from interviews currently paying $100+/month
□ Clear, consistent pain points you can solve
□ Validation cost: $300-500 (on budget)

CONFIDENCE LEVEL: 80%+
ACTION: Proceed to building MVP
TIMELINE: Start building immediately
RISK: Low - market demand proven
```

**⚠️ MEDIUM GO - Proceed with Caution:**

```
□ 5-9 people pre-paid $99
□ 30-50 email signups
□ 10-14 people currently paying $100+/month
□ Some pain points but not super consistent
□ People interested but not enthusiastic

CONFIDENCE LEVEL: 50-70%
ACTION: Do 10 more interviews + refine pitch, try again
OR: Build small MVP and test with 5 pre-paid customers first
TIMELINE: 2 more weeks of validation OR start cautiously
RISK: Medium - some demand but not overwhelming
```

**❌ NO-GO - Don't Build This:**

```
□ <5 people pre-paid $99
□ <30 email signups
□ <10 people from interviews paying $100+/month
□ No clear pain points or people happy with current solution
□ Lots of "maybe" but no money

CONFIDENCE LEVEL: <50%
ACTION: Pivot to job hunting immediately
TIMELINE: Stop SaaS, start job applications this week
RISK: High - weak demand signals
```

## The Decision

### **Scenario 1: Strong GO + No Job Offers**

```
✅ Market validation succeeded
✅ 10+ people paid you $99
✅ Clear demand signals

ACTION:
□ Email the 10+ people who pre-paid: "Starting development"
□ Set launch date (6 months out)
□ Begin building MVP
□ Budget remaining runway: 6-12 months
□ Set milestone: If <50 customers by Month 6, reassess

RISKS:
- Still 18-24 months to profitability
- Could run out of money
- Job market could tighten

MITIGATION:
- Keep job applications warm (1-2/week)
- Set hard checkpoints every 3 months
- Be ready to pivot if traction is slow
```

---

### **Scenario 2: Strong GO + Good Job Offers**

```
✅ Market validation succeeded
✅ Have 1-2 job offers

THIS IS THE HARDEST DECISION:

Option A: Take job, build SaaS on side
├─ Pros: Stable income, low risk, can still pursue dream
├─ Cons: Slower progress (2 years vs 1 year), less focused
└─ Best if: Job is good, you're risk-averse, or not confident

Option B: Turn down job, build SaaS full-time
├─ Pros: Faster progress, fully committed, could work
├─ Cons: Risky, 18 months to profitability, could fail
└─ Best if: Strong conviction, enough runway, high risk tolerance

RECOMMENDATION:
If job pays $80K+: Take it, build SaaS nights/weekends
If job pays $60K or less: Consider going all-in on SaaS
If you have 12 months runway: Maybe go all-in
If you have 6 months runway: Take the job (too risky)
```

---

### **Scenario 3: Medium Validation + Any Job Situation**

```
⚠️ Validation was lukewarm
⚠️ 5-9 pre-sales, okay signals

ACTION:
□ If you have job offer: TAKE IT
□ Build small MVP on side with the 5-9 people who paid
□ See if it actually gets traction before quitting job
□ Give it 6 months on the side
□ Reassess at Month 6

This is the SAFE path when signals are mixed
```

---

### **Scenario 4: NO-GO**

```
❌ Validation failed
❌ <5 pre-sales, weak signals

ACTION:
□ STOP all SaaS work immediately
□ Focus 100% on job hunting
□ You spent 2 months and $500 max
□ You still have 10 months runway
□ You learned validation BEFORE wasting 12 months

LESSONS LEARNED:
□ Market doesn't want this at this price
□ Boutique segment not willing to pay
□ PHPCollab brand not strong enough
□ Competition too entrenched

NEXT STEPS:
□ Apply to 10 jobs per week
□ Broaden search (adjacent roles, contract work)
□ Consider relocation if needed
□ Accept first reasonable offer

You made the RIGHT decision to validate first
Most founders waste 12-24 months before learning this
You learned it in 2 months for $500
```

## Emotional Checkpoint

**This is hard. Check in with yourself:**

```
End of Week 2:
□ Am I getting demoralized by rejections?
□ Am I staying disciplined?
□ Am I learning useful things?
□ Is my financial situation okay?

End of Week 4:
□ Do I still believe in this?
□ Are the signals encouraging or discouraging?
□ Am I being honest with myself about results?
□ Do I need to pivot to jobs?

End of Week 6:
□ Can I afford to keep going?
□ Are people actually paying?
□ Do I have job options as backup?
□ What does my gut tell me?

End of Week 8 (Decision Time):
□ Am I making this decision based on data or emotion?
□ Can I accept walking away if validation failed?
□ Am I being realistic about runway?
□ What would I tell a friend in my situation?
```

**Remember:**
- Validation failure is GOOD - saves you 12 months
- Finding out fast is the goal, not being right
- Getting a job is not failure, it's smart
- You can always try again later with stability

## The Bottom Line

**Success = Making a data-driven decision by Week 8**

✅ **You succeeded if:**
- You talked to 30 people
- You built a landing page
- You tried to get pre-sales
- You made GO/NO-GO decision based on data
- You didn't waste all 12 months on hope

❌ **You failed if:**
- You skipped validation and just started building
- You ignored weak signals and kept going
- You ran out of money before getting job
- You let emotions override data

**The goal isn't to prove the idea works.**
**The goal is to find out the truth as fast and cheap as possible.**

**If validation works: Great! Build it.**
**If validation fails: Great! You saved 12 months and still have runway for job.**

**Either way, you made a smart, data-driven decision.**
